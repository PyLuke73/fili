fili - read before installing / leggere prima di installare
=============================================================

This installer only copies files (fili.exe, fili-gui.exe, wdi-simple.exe, licenses). It does NOT change any
driver and does NOT add any certificate to this PC. L'installazione copia solo file: non cambia driver e non
aggiunge certificati.

WHEN YOU USE fili (not now):
  * Installing a WinUSB / libusbK / libusb-win32 driver creates a SELF-SIGNED certificate and adds it to the
    machine's Root and TrustedPublisher stores. Anything signed with it is then trusted by this PC.
    "fili cert-remove" lists and removes those certificates.
  * It needs Administrator rights and replaces the driver of the device you select. A wrong target can disable
    your keyboard, mouse, disk or Bluetooth (fili refuses those classes unless you pass --force).
  * Use it only on devices you own. "fili restore" undoes a driver change.
  * Quando lo usi (non ora): crea un certificato AUTOFIRMATO nei repository Root e TrustedPublisher del PC e
    sostituisce il driver del dispositivo scelto. Usalo solo su dispositivi tuoi; "fili restore" annulla.
